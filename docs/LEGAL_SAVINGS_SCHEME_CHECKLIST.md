## 1. Regulatory Framework & Deposit Restrictions

### 1.1. Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act, 2019 (BUDS Act)
- **Question for Lawyer:** Does our advance jewelry booking model qualify for the statutory exemption under the BUDS Act as an *"advance received in connection with and held for the purpose of an offer or promise of supply of goods"*?
- **Question for Lawyer:** What specific contractual language must appear on the customer booking agreement and advance receipt voucher to establish that funds received are strictly commercial advances for jewelry purchase and NOT a financial deposit?
- **Question for CA:** If the store provides a promotional concession (e.g., 50% discount on making charges or showroom incentive upon completion of 11 installments), does this risk reclassification as an unauthorized "return" or "interest" under the BUDS Act?

### 1.2. Companies Act, 2013 & Acceptance of Deposits Rules
- **Question for CA/Lawyer:** What is the legal entity type of Ambika Jewels (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership Firm, LLP, or Private Limited Company)?
- **Question for Lawyer:** Under the Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules, an advance for goods is exempt from deposit definitions ONLY IF the goods are supplied within a maximum of **365 days** from the date of acceptance.
  - Can we legally enforce an 11-month or 12-month tenure with a strict maturity cutoff mandating jewelry delivery within 365 days?
  - If structured as an unincorporated proprietorship or partnership, does the entity face any local J&K money-lending or deposit acceptance prohibitions?

### 1.3. Chit Funds Act, 1982 & Prize Chits Prohibition
- **Question for Lawyer:** How do we legally ensure the scheme cannot be deemed a "chit fund" or "prize chit"?
  - Confirm that zero lucky draws, random allotments, or tiered bonus schemes are permitted.
  - Confirm that 100% of customers receive physical BIS hallmarked jewelry matching their exact accumulated rupee/gold weight value.

## 2. Goods & Services Tax (GST) Treatment of Advances

### 2.1. Time of Supply & Advance Receipts (CGST Act)
- **Question for CA:** Under Notification No. 66/2017-Central Tax, registered suppliers of goods are exempted from paying GST at the time of receipt of advances (tax is payable upon issuance of the final tax invoice when goods are delivered).
  - Does this exemption unequivocally apply to retail precious jewelry advance schemes where weight/price may be fixed or floating?
  - Does fixing the gold rate at each monthly installment change the GST characterization from an advance for goods to a commodity forward contract?

### 2.2. Documentation & Receipt Vouchers
- **Question for CA:** Are we legally mandated to issue a formal **GST Receipt Voucher** (under Section 31(3)(d) of the CGST Act) containing:
  - Customer Name, Address, and State Code
  - Place of Supply (J&K vs Other States)
  - Nature of Goods (HSN 7113 - Gold/Diamond Jewelry)
  - Rate of Tax (3% GST)
- **Question for CA:** When the final jewelry piece is selected at maturity, how should the advance receipt vouchers be adjusted against the final Tax Invoice?

## 3. Cash Payment Restrictions & Prevention of Money Laundering

### 3.1. Cash Acceptance Limits (Income Tax Compliance)
- **Question for CA:** Tax laws strictly regulate accepting ₹2,00,000 or more in cash:
  - In an installment scheme where each monthly installment is ₹15,000 in cash, but the cumulative order value exceeds ₹2,00,000, does accepting cash monthly violate the rule against accepting ₹2,00,000 or more in aggregate for a single transaction or related transactions?
  - Should the store policy mandate that all advance installment payments exceeding ₹10,000 per month must be paid via **UPI, Net Banking (NEFT/RTGS), or Debit/Credit Card**?

### 3.2. Mandatory Customer PAN & Form 60
- **Question for CA:** At what milestone must customer PAN be collected?
  - At the initiation of the advance booking agreement, or at the time of final invoice issuance when total exceeds ₹2,00,000?
  - Can Form 60 be accepted in lieu of PAN for advance gold bookings from agriculturalists or non-PAN holders?
