## 👑 Brand Overview & Showroom Features

Established in 2021 in Jammu, Ambika Jewels is dedicated to offering premium quality hallmarked gold, certified diamond, and 925 sterling silver jewelry. The business is renowned for its **Signature Dogra Heritage Collection**, reflecting the cultural heritage of the Jammu region.

Alongside the main flagship jewelry showroom, Ambika Jewels operates an exclusive **Boutique managed directly by owner Shivani Anand**, providing personalized service to local, NRI, and bridal customers.

### Key Brand Features & USPs
1. **Exclusive & Unique Designs**: Handcrafted patterns not commonly available elsewhere.
2. **Authentic Dogra Specialty**: Regionally authentic heritage jewelry crafted by master Jammu karigars.
3. **Multi-Purity Gold**: Available in 22K (916), 18K (750), 14K (585), and 9K (375) Hallmarked Gold.
4. **Certified Real Diamonds**: 18K and 14K gold diamond settings accompanied by official GIA & IGI certificates.
5. **925 Hallmarked Silver**: Fine sterling silver jewelry and traditional silver ornaments.
6. **Gold Exchange & Melting Program**: 100% valuation gold exchange and old gold melting into modern designs.
7. **3D CAD Customization**: Custom jewelry preview generated within 2 days from any sketch or photo shared on WhatsApp.

## 💎 Products & Collections

The showroom offers a complete range of fine jewelry categorized as follows:

### 1. Signature Dogra Heritage Collection
- **Dogri Jhumki**: Traditional Jammu umbrella silhouette Jhumki crafted in 22K gold with seed pearl droplets.
- **Dogri Naman Set**: Sacred 22K gold Dogri Naman necklace set with floral relief medallions and natural Burmese rubies.
- **Dogri Long Set**: Multi-strand 22K gold long bridal Haar with Meenakari enamel backing.
- **Dogra Heritage Relics**: Authentic traditional sets representing Jammu’s cultural legacy.

### 2. Gold Jewelry (Multi-Purity Options)
- **22K Gold (916)**: Traditional Guluband chokers, Rani Haars, bangles, and bridal suites.
- **18K Gold (750)**: Designer chains, pendant sets, and modern engagement rings.
- **14K Gold (585)**: Lightweight daily wear chains, sleek bangles, and office wear.
- **9K Gold (375)**: Affordable solid gold stackable bands and minimalist wear.

### 3. Certified Diamond Jewelry
- **18K & 14K Gold Diamond Settings**: Certified VVS1 solitaire engagement rings, emerald-cut diamond studs, and diamond tennis bracelets with official GIA/IGI certificates.

### 4. 925 Sterling Silver Collection
- **925 Hallmarked Silver**: Hand-hammered tribal chokers, Dogri style silver payal pairs, silver coins, and traditional silver artifacts.

### 5. Specialized Categories
- **Bridal Couture**: Royal Kundan choker sets, Polki Haathphool, Mathapatti, and full wedding trousseaus.
- **Men's Accessories**: Heavy solid 22K gold Kadas, royal lion insignia cufflinks, and gold kurta buttons.
- **Everyday Wear & Minimalist**: Lightweight chains, pearl bracelets, and daily wear diamond pendants.
