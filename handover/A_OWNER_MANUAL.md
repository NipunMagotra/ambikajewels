## 2. Updating Daily Gold & Silver Rates

Every morning when the Jammu Bullion Association announces the opening market rate, update the rates on your dashboard. This ensures online prices are always accurate and protects you from selling below market value.

1. In the admin dashboard, click the **📈 Bullion &amp; Pricing** tab.
2. In the **Jammu Bullion Market Rates** form, enter the latest rates in Rupees per gram:
   - **Gold 24K (999 Fineness):** e.g., `7850`
   - **Gold 22K (916 Hallmarked):** e.g., `7190`
   - **Gold 18K (750 Hallmarked):** e.g., `5890`
   - **Gold 14K (585 Everyday Gold):** e.g., `4580`
   - **Silver 999 Fine (Chandi):** e.g., `98`
   - **Silver 925 Sterling:** e.g., `85`
3. Click **Publish &amp; Lock Daily Bullion Rates**.
4. A green confirmation banner will appear. All products with *Live Bullion Formula* pricing will automatically recalculate their prices across the entire website instantly.

> [!NOTE]
> If you enter a rate that is more than **10% higher or lower** than yesterday's rate, the system will ask you to confirm to prevent accidental typing mistakes.

## 3. Adding a New Jewellery Piece

To showcase a new necklace, ring, bangle, or bridal set on your website:

1. Click the **💎 Jewellery Pieces** tab.
2. Click the golden **+ Add Jewellery Piece** button in the top right.
3. A guided window will open with 4 simple steps:

### Step 1: Basic Information
- **Product Name:** e.g., *Royal Dogri 22K Gold Bridal Naman*
- **Category:** Select from the dropdown (Necklaces, Earrings, Bangles, Rings, Mangalsutra, etc.).
- **Collection Group:** e.g., *Dogra Heritage Collection*, *Bridal Trousseau*, or *Everyday Elegance*.
- **Description:** Plain English description describing the craftsmanship and heritage value.
- **Display Badges:** e.g., `22K BIS, DOGRA HERITAGE, BESTSELLER`.

### Step 2: Gold & Purity Specifications
- **Purity:** Select *22K (916)*, *18K (750)*, *14K*, or *925 Sterling Silver*.
- **Net Metal Weight (Grams):** Enter the actual gold weight on your electronic scale (excluding stones and wax).
- **Gross Weight (Grams):** Total weight with stones.
- **BIS Hallmark 6-Character HUID:** Enter the unique 6-character laser code engraved on the piece (e.g. `AB12CD`).
- **BIS Hallmarked Piece:** Keep checked so customers see the certified BIS emblem.
- **Dimensions:** Enter length, breadth, and height in centimetres.
- **Sizing Variants:** If a ring or bangle, enter available sizes (e.g., `12, 14, 16` or `2.4, 2.6`).

### Step 3: Photos & Gallery
- Click **Click to Upload Jewellery Photos** to select photos from your device, or drag images directly from your folder.
- Images upload automatically to secure storage.
- You can hover over any photo to click **★ Set Cover** (making it the main product image) or click the trash can to delete a photo.

### Step 4: Pricing & Stock
- **Select Pricing Method:**
  - **Live Bullion Formula (Recommended for Gold):** Prices update automatically every day when you update gold rates. Enter your Making Charge (e.g. `8%` or `₹450/gram`) and Gemstone value (if any). The live preview will calculate the exact customer price including **3% GST**.
  - **Fixed Catalog Price:** Use this for silver items, gift articles, or fixed-price catalog pieces. Enter the final price in Rupees.
- **Stock Availability:** Choose *In Stock*, *Limited Stock (1-2 left)*, or *Sold Out*.
- **Feature on Homepage:** Check this if you want the piece highlighted on the website homepage.
- Click **Publish Product**. The piece is now live on your website!

## 4. Editing, Duplicating & Deleting Products

- **To Edit:** In the **💎 Jewellery Pieces** tab, find the item and click the **Edit** button. Modify any field and click **Save Changes**.
- **To Duplicate:** Want to add a similar ring or bangle in a different weight without retyping everything? Click the **📋 Copy** button. A duplicate named `(Copy)` is created instantly.
- **To Delete:** Click the red **🗑** button and confirm. The piece will be removed from your catalog.

## 5. Bulk Price & Stock Adjustments

When market conditions change or you want to run a seasonal sale across multiple pieces:

1. In the **💎 Jewellery Pieces** tab, check the boxes next to the items you want to change (or click the top box to select all visible pieces).
2. A golden bar will appear at the top saying e.g. *5 pieces selected*.
3. Click **⚡ Bulk Update Prices / Stock**.
4. Choose your action:
   - **% Price Adjust:** e.g. enter `5` to increase prices by 5%, or `-5` to offer a 5% promotional discount.
   - **₹ Fixed Adjust:** e.g. add or subtract ₹2,000 across all selected items.
   - **Stock Availability:** Mark all selected pieces as *In Stock* or *Sold Out* in one click.
   - **Move Category:** Shift pieces into another collection.
5. Click **Apply**. All selected pieces are updated together with an audit record created automatically.

## 7. Fulfilling Customer Orders & BVC Logistics

When a customer places an order on [ambikajewelsonline.com](https://ambikajewelsonline.com):

1. Click the **🛍️ Orders** tab.
2. Locate the order by order number (e.g. `AJ-20261110-001`) or customer name.
3. Click **Details ▼** to expand the order drawer.
4. Verify that **Payment Status** shows green **PAID** (Razorpay captured).
5. Advance the **Order Lifecycle Status** as your workshop proceeds:
   - **Confirmed:** You have verified the order and set aside the inventory.
   - **Processing:** Goldsmith is sizing or polishing the piece.
   - **Shipped:** Handed over to BVC Logistics armed courier.
   - **Delivered:** Customer has received and signed for the package.
6. When BVC Logistics issues your tracking airway bill (AWB):
   - Enter the docket number in the **BVC Logistics Airway Bill / Docket Number** field.
   - Click **Save**. The customer can now track their parcel on your website under `/track`.

## 8. Printing Official GST Tax Invoices

Ambika Jewels complies with **Section 31 of the CGST Act 2017** and **Rule 114B of the Income Tax Act**.

1. In the **🛍️ Orders** tab, click the golden **🧾 Print GST Bill** button on any order.
2. An official, beautifully formatted **Tax Invoice (Form GST INV-1)** appears on screen containing:
   - Ambika Jewels GSTIN (`01AHWPH9511N1ZX`), PAN (`AHWPH9511N`), and BIS License (`HM/C-9990041009`).
   - Customer shipping address, phone, and PAN (if order exceeds ₹2 Lakh).
   - HSN Code `7113` for precious jewellery.
   - Complete tax breakdown: 1.5% CGST + 1.5% SGST (or 3% IGST).
   - Statutory BIS Hallmarking and 6-character HUID declaration.
   - Grand total in Indian numbering words (e.g., *One Lakh Eighty-Five Thousand Rupees Only*).
3. Click **Print Invoice** in the top right. It is formatted to print cleanly on standard A4 paper.

## 9. Printing Workshop Packing Slips

Before handing the precious jewellery to your courier or workshop:

1. In the **🛍️ Orders** tab, click the blue **📦 Packing Slip** button.
2. A dedicated workshop dispatch sheet appears with:
   - Order number and delivery destination.
   - Exact jewellery item names, metal finish, and selected sizes.
   - Pre-Dispatch Vault Inspection Checklist:
     - [ ] BIS Hallmarking &amp; 6-digit HUID laser confirmed
     - [ ] Jewellery Authenticity Certificate Card enclosed
     - [ ] Luxury velvet presentation box enclosed
     - [ ] BVC Tamper-Proof Security Pouch heat-sealed
   - BVC security pouch barcode and packer signature lines.
3. Click **Print Packing Slip** to print a copy for your showroom records.

## 10. Creating & Managing Discount Coupons

1. Click the **🎟️ Coupons** tab.
2. Click **+ Create Promo Code**.
3. Fill in the simple form:
   - **Coupon Code:** e.g., `DIWALI2026` or `DOGRA5000` (automatically converted to uppercase).
   - **Discount Type:** Choose *₹ Flat Rupee Off* (e.g. ₹5,000 off) or *% Percentage Off* (e.g. 2% off).
   - **Minimum Order Value:** e.g. `50000` (coupon only works if cart is at least ₹50,000).
   - **Valid Until:** Choose the expiration date from the calendar.
   - **Description:** Plain English description shown to the customer.
4. Click **Save Coupon**.
5. To temporarily pause a coupon without deleting it, click the green **Active** badge in the table to toggle it to **Disabled**.

## 12. Reviewing Store Activity & Audit Logs

For complete transparency and security, the system maintains a permanent audit trail of every change made in the admin console.

1. Click the **📋 Audit Log** tab.
2. You will see a chronological ledger showing:
   - **When:** Exact date and time of the change.
   - **Who:** Staff member email and name.
   - **Action:** e.g. `PRODUCT_CREATED`, `BULLION_RATES_UPDATED`, `ORDER_UPDATED`, `COUPON_CREATED`.
   - **Item:** The exact product, order, or coupon affected.
   - **IP Address:** Location of the device used.
3. Click **🔄 Refresh Logs** anytime to view the latest entries.
