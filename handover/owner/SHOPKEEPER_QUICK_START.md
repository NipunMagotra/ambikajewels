# Ambika Jewels — Shopkeeper 1-Page Quick Start Guide

**Print this sheet and keep it at your showroom billing counter.**

---

### 1. Opening the Admin Console (Morning)
1. Turn on your counter computer or open your tablet browser.
2. Go to: **`ambikajewelsonline.com/admin`**
3. Enter your secret **Admin Passcode** and click **Sign In**.
4. Keep the tab open during showroom business hours (10:00 AM to 8:00 PM).

---

### 2. Updating Gold Rates in 30 Seconds
**Every morning after Jammu market rate opens:**
1. Click **📈 Bullion &amp; Pricing** at the top.
2. Enter today's **24K Gold** (e.g. `7850`) and **22K Gold** (e.g. `7190`) per gram.
3. Enter today's **Silver** rate (e.g. `98` / `85`).
4. Click **Publish &amp; Lock Daily Bullion Rates**.
*Done! All live gold prices across the entire website update instantly.*

---

### 3. Fulfilling an Online Customer Order (3 Steps)
When an order arrives:
1. Click **🛍️ Orders** and check for **Pending Confirmation** status.
2. Click **🧾 Print GST Bill** → print 1 copy for customer package and 1 copy for showroom filing.
3. Click **📦 Packing Slip** → take to workshop to verify piece weight, BIS hallmark, and seal into BVC security pouch.
4. When handed to BVC courier: Enter the **BVC Docket Number** in the box and change status to **Shipped**.

---

### 4. Over-the-Counter Showroom Sale? Mark Sold Out in 1 Click!
If a customer buys a unique heritage piece physically in your Jammu showroom:
1. Click **📦 Inventory** at the top.
2. Find the piece name.
3. Click the red **Sold Out** button on that row.
*Done! The website immediately marks it as Sold Out so no online buyer can purchase the same item.*

---

### 5. Need to Change a Price or Photo?
1. Click **💎 Jewellery Pieces**.
2. Find the piece and click **Edit**.
3. Change the weight, price, or upload a new photo.
4. Click **Save Changes**.
