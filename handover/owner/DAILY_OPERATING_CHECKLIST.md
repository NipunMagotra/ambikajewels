# Ambika Jewels — Daily Operating Checklist

This checklist provides a structured morning and evening procedure for the showroom manager and staff operating `ambikajewelsonline.com`.

---

## ☀️ MORNING CHECKLIST (10:00 AM – 10:30 AM)

- [ ] **1. Internet & Device Ready**  
  Power on counter PC or tablet. Ensure high-speed Wi-Fi is connected.
- [ ] **2. Log In to Admin Console**  
  Visit `ambikajewelsonline.com/admin` and sign in with the daily passcode.
- [ ] **3. Bullion Market Rate Update**  
  Check opening rates from the Jammu Bullion Association:
  - Update **24K Gold**, **22K Gold**, **18K Gold**, **14K Gold**, and **Silver**.
  - Click **Publish & Lock Daily Bullion Rates**.
  - Verify that the rate ticker on the top header displays the correct figures.
- [ ] **4. Check Overnight Orders**  
  Navigate to the **🛍️ Orders** tab:
  - Review orders placed between 8:00 PM yesterday and 10:00 AM today.
  - Verify that Razorpay payment status is marked green **PAID**.
  - If transaction is greater than ₹2,00,000, ensure customer PAN is captured on the invoice (Rule 114B).
- [ ] **5. Print Invoices & Packing Slips**  
  - For each new order, print the **GST Tax Invoice** and **Workshop Packing Slip**.
  - Hand over packing slips to the master goldsmith for weight and hallmark verification.
- [ ] **6. Arrange BVC Courier Pickup**  
  - Call or log into the BVC Logistics portal to schedule afternoon vault pickup for packed orders.

---

## 🌤️ MID-DAY CHECKLIST (2:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

- [ ] **7. Inspection of Packed Jewellery**  
  - Weigh each finished piece on the digital scale and verify against the packing slip.
  - Inspect the 6-character laser HUID under magnification.
  - Enclose the Jewellery Authenticity Certificate Card and velvet box.
  - Heat-seal into the tamper-proof BVC Logistics security bag.
- [ ] **8. Courier Handover & Docket Entry**  
  - Hand over sealed security bags to the armed BVC logistics representative upon arrival.
  - Obtain the signed pickup manifest and consignment barcode docket.
  - In the admin console, enter the **BVC Docket Number** into the order drawer and change status to **Shipped**.
- [ ] **9. Check Customer Enquiries**  
  - Check the showroom WhatsApp (+91 9086098457 / +91 9682589725) and email for bespoke design or sizing queries.

---

## 🌙 EVENING CLOSING CHECKLIST (7:30 PM – 8:00 PM)

- [ ] **10. Reconcile Over-the-Counter Sales with Online Stock**  
  - Review all physical jewellery sold from showroom display cases today.
  - In the **📦 Inventory** tab, click **Sold Out** for any unique or single-piece items that were sold in the showroom to avoid double selling online.
- [ ] **11. Review Razorpay Payment Settlements**  
  - Open Razorpay Dashboard (`dashboard.razorpay.com`).
  - Verify that today's captured online payments match the total in your admin **Overview** tab.
  - Check scheduled bank transfer / settlement time (typically T+1 or T+2 working days).
- [ ] **12. Review Store Management Audit Log**  
  - Click the **📋 Audit Log** tab.
  - Quick scan to confirm all rate changes, price updates, and status modifications made today were authorized.
- [ ] **13. Secure Logout**  
  - Click **Log Out** in the top-right corner of the admin header to terminate the active 12-hour session.
  - Lock counter computer.
