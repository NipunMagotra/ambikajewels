# Ambika Jewels — Design System & Visual Direction

## Brand Identity & Soul
Ambika Jewels is a heritage fine jewellery house established in Jammu, J&K. The brand bridges regal Dogra dynasty heirloom artistry (Dogri Jhumkis, Naman sets, Royal Kundan & Polki) with contemporary 22K/18K fine jewellery and certified natural diamonds.

## Aesthetic Direction: Royal Heritage & Warm Editorial Luxury
The visual language evokes the intimacy, trust, and grandeur of a private high-jewelry salon in northern India.

### 1. Palette System
- **Canvas / Background**: 
  - Light mode: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Alabaster / Champagne Ivory)
  - Dark mode: `#120908` (Deep Velvet Espresso / Royal Obsidian)
- **Surfaces & Cards**:
  - Light mode: `#F5EFEB` (Cashmere Linen / Warm Sand)
  - Dark mode: `#1C100F` (Dark Rosewood / Burnished Mahogany)
- **Royal Accent**:
  - Light mode: `#4A121A` (Imperial Burgundy / Rajput Wine Velvet)
  - Dark mode: `#8C2232` (Luminous Ruby Velvet)
- **Precious Metal Gold**:
  - Light mode: `#9E7A23` (Antique 22K Muted Gold) & `#B8860B` (Burnished Gold Leaf)
  - Dark mode: `#D8B75A` (Radiant 22K Gold) & `#E8CC70` (Polished Gold Leaf)
- **Borders & Hairlines**:
  - Subtle gold filament: `rgba(158, 122, 35, 0.18)`
  - Card borders: `rgba(158, 122, 35, 0.22)`
- **Shadows**:
  - Warm elevation: `0 4px 24px rgba(30, 20, 18, 0.06)`

### 2. Typography
- **Headings & Titles**: `Cormorant Garamond` and `Playfair Display` (graceful serifs, optical character sizes, dignified royal cadence).
- **Body & Specs**: `Inter` (high-clarity modern sans, optimal legibility for karat purity, gram weights, and statutory Indian invoicing).
- **Letter Spacing**: Generous uppercase tracking (`tracking-[0.25em]` to `tracking-[0.35em]`) for luxury category badges and brand markers.

### 3. Anti-Slop Dials
- **ENERGY**: 2 — Confident, stately, and tactile. Never frantic or crowded.
- **RHYTHM**: 3 — Rich structural variety. Asymmetrical editorial spreads, circular craft rings, full-width heirloom spotlights, and clean product grids. No repetitive bento-grid templates.
- **MOTION**: 2 — Restrained, purposeful micro-interactions. Slow 700ms image zooms on hover, gold hairline reveals, zero endless pulses or bouncing loops.

### 4. Content & Honesty
- Authentic Dogra craft terminology (Dogri Jhumkis, Naman sets, Chaap, Polki, Kundan, Payal).
- No em dashes (`—`) in copy.
- Real statutory disclosures (100% BIS Hallmarked Gold, 6-digit HUID laser inscription, natural certified diamonds, BVC armored logistics).
- Direct WhatsApp Karigar consultation and interactive Indian ring/bangle sizing.
